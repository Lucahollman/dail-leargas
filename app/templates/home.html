Dáil Léargas

The Dáil Léargas project is a website that allows users to explore the spoken record of Dáil Éireann. The site includes various debate-level, TD-level, party-level, and word-level statistics, including word frequencies, language-use estimates, sentiment scores, and other descriptive measures. The project aims to make parliamentary proceedings more accessible to people who may not have the time or inclination to read through tedious Dáil transcripts and allow users to get a better sense of what is being discussed within Dáil Éireann. Created by Luca Hollman.

About the website

You can read more about how we generate our statistics here.

This project uses data provided by the Houses of the Oireachtas under the Oireachtas (Public Sector Information – Open Data) Licence.

All statistics displayed are estimates generated from Oireachtas data and do not represent official Oireachtas interpretations.

Official records are available at oireachtas.ie.
